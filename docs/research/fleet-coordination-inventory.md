## Cross-cutting reading (what the table says as a whole)

- **Orchestra's delivery honesty is per-hop and observational** (`'started'` confirmations, park-don't-drop), while orca's is **transactional and replayed** (ack-or-redeliver). Everything in rows 1–3 that took a wave to fix (#57's silent drop, R1's phantom rows, R2's double delivery) is a hazard orca's `messages`/`deliveries`/ack design excludes by construction — at the cost of at-least-once semantics Orchestra currently only has on the inbox leg.
- **Ordering does not exist anywhere in Orchestra's channel** and every ledger §Briefing is a manual workaround for that; orca's sequence AUTOINCREMENT is the single cheapest-looking adoption with the widest blast radius on the protocol (it would obsolete "pings are pings" as a rule).
- **"Done" authority** is Orchestra's weakest surface (row 4: four distinct field failures, two of them false NOT LANDED, one destroyed a running agent's workspace); orca's capability tokens + task state machine answer authority, but NOT the squash-merge/content question — `verify-landed` stays necessary even on a bus.
- **Liveness**: rows 6–8 are three partial, absence-based liveness detectors (socket state, gate silence, stall age); orca has one positive-signal contract (heartbeat). The #88/#90 comments themselves document how much guard-engineering absence-based detection costs.
- The **protocol rules that must survive any substrate** (per the ledger scars): pull-before-act, NOT VERIFIED lists, prove-can-fail gates, single-writer sections. orca's injected preamble is the mechanism for making those ride along mechanically.
